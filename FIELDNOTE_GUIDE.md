# Field-note Guide

- Instrument version: `0.1.0-draft`
- Status: `DRAFT`
- Fieldwork authorization: none

Field notes are situated records of researcher observation and reflexive practice. They are not life histories, transcripts, participant dossiers, or neutral mirrors of events.

## Required methodological distinctions

A field-note instrument must provide separate sections for:

- observation;
- participant account, if ethically collected and relevant;
- researcher interpretation;
- analytic memo;
- researcher role and position;
- access conditions;
- changes in the field relationship;
- interpretive uncertainty; and
- methodological changes.

Observation scope, time precision, and place precision must be limited to what is methodologically necessary and ethically safe. Public methodological material must not expose sensitive location/time combinations.

## Reflexive practice

Researchers should record how access, role, expectations, power, language, mediation, and relationships shape what can be encountered and interpreted. Later interpretations must not overwrite the original distinction between observation and inference.

## Storage boundary

This public repository contains only the blank schema and methodological guidance. Raw or identifiable field notes require an approved non-public storage and access-control plan and must never be committed here.
