# Governance

- Instrument version: `0.1.0-draft`
- Status: `DRAFT`
- Fieldwork authorization: none

## Authority

No responsible researcher, ethics reviewer, disclosure reviewer, or institutional authority is named or implied by this scaffold. Roles, conflicts, quorum, delegation, recusal, and succession remain unresolved.

## Controlled instruments

The protocol, field-note guide, life-history protocol, ethics and data-governance framework, readiness schema, release schema, and methodological schemas are versioned instruments. Changes must identify rationale, effective date, and comparability or participant-protection impact.

## Fail-closed decisions

Unresolved readiness means fieldwork is not authorized. Missing release review means participant-derived publication is not authorized. A repository maintainer must not infer approval from silence, prior practice, a passing syntax check, or access to data.

## Privacy precedence

Normal scientific records should preserve provenance. That principle is subordinate to participant privacy, withdrawal commitments, and ethical or legal duties. Incident response may delete content and rewrite history. Safe documentation of the remediation decision should be retained only when it does not reproduce the disclosure.

## Independence

The Observatory and Anthropology repositories are governed independently. A technology-side observation, lock, approval, or release does not authorize human-subject research or publication here.
