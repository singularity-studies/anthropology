# Life-history Protocol

- Instrument version: `0.1.0-draft`
- Status: `DRAFT`
- Fieldwork authorization: none

Life histories trace lived change over time. They are conceptually distinct from field notes, which record situated observation and researcher reflection.

## Temporal representation

The life-history schema records whether its temporal basis is longitudinal, retrospective single encounter, or another explicitly described design. Only a longitudinal design may claim repeated engagement, and it requires at least two engagement dates. A single encounter must remain labeled as a single encounter.

## Methodological separation

A life-history instrument keeps observation, participant account, researcher interpretation, and analytic memo separate. It also records researcher position, access conditions, field-relationship changes, interpretive uncertainty, and methodological changes.

## Interpretation

Accounts may contain participants’ own causal interpretations. Those accounts must not be silently converted into researcher claims that AI caused a change or that a “Singularity” occurred. Resistance, non-adoption, reversal, ambivalence, and re-humanization remain valid trajectories.

## Storage boundary

This repository contains no life history. Any future collection involving people requires fieldwork readiness, appropriate consent or lawful alternative basis, controlled non-public storage, withdrawal procedures, and disclosure review.
